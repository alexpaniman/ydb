F02 — Aggregate interval SUM uses the wrong accumulator representation

Correctness: the new aggregate lowering emits an unsupported Interval64
AggrAdd call. For the smaller Interval type it also checks the interval range
while accumulating, so an intermediate overflow can lose part of a sum even
when later values would bring the final total back into range. Ordinary and
DISTINCT SUM share this lowering. The Interval64 compilation failure is
natively confirmed; overflow cancellation is source-confirmed and awaits the
generated native controls.

Narrow fix: normalize interval SUM inputs through Int64 to Decimal(35,0) in
the early RBO aggregate stage, reuse existing Decimal aggregation and partial
merging, then convert the completed result through Int64 to its original
interval type. Preserve result IDs, grouping keys, DISTINCT and final NULL
on overflow. No yql/essentials changes. Bounded physical windows use the
separate F03 fix.
