Correctness: the general logical-subtree copy policy admits SUM, MIN, MAX and
DISTINCT by function name while explicitly ignoring argument types and reduction
order. Separate evaluations can disagree: floating sums depend on order, bounded
or saturating sums can lose recoverable intermediates, and equal floating or
timezone values can retain different observable representations. Later casts
or serialization can hide the unstable source behind integer or string outputs.
